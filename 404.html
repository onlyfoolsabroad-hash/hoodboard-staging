02 — The rule

Six hours
above the line.

Touching a number is not making the Board. A coin joins a tier only when it stays above that tier's line — continuously, on its canonical market. Fall back and it drops immediately.

05 — Replay

Rewind the day.

The Board is a state, so it can be rebuilt at any minute of the last 24 hours. Scrub back and watch coins climb, qualify, make a tier — and come off it.

06 — What it won't do

The refusals
are the product.

Most of the work went into what the Board declines to say. A tool that fills its gaps with plausible guesses is worse than useless in a market like this one.

✕
It won't call a coin safe, legit, or a scam. It reports what held and what did not. Judgement stays with you.
✕
No language model decides the Board. Qualification is a fixed rule you can read.
✕
An FDV is never called a market cap. Where no defensible circulating supply exists, it says FDV.
✕
Gaps are never filled with estimates. What the chain can't support is shown as absent, with the reason.
